Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains how SoftFlow handles information when you use the SoftFlow desktop application and related services.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, SoftFlow may process account details, information you choose to store in the application (such as notes, calendar entries and preferences), and technical information needed to operate and protect the service.

2. How information is used

Information is used to provide application features, synchronise supported services, maintain accounts, troubleshoot issues, protect the service and respond to support requests.

3. Google Calendar

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, SoftFlow uses Google authorisation to access the calendar information permitted by the permissions you approve. Calendar information is used to provide the integration features you request. You can disconnect the integration through the application or revoke access through your Google Account settings.

4. Storage and service providers

SoftFlow uses third-party infrastructure and service providers, including Supabase, for certain application functions. Information processed by these providers is subject to their applicable privacy and security practices. The exact data stored and processing arrangements should be confirmed against the current production configuration.

5. Sharing

SoftFlow does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate the application, or disclosed where required by law or to protect the service and its users.

6. Security and retention

Reasonable measures are used to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Information is retained only as needed for the purposes described in this policy and applicable legal requirements.

7. Your choices and rights

You may contact SoftFlow to ask about access, correction or deletion of personal information. You can also manage connected Google access through your Google Account.

8. Children's privacy

SoftFlow is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as SoftFlow develops. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact softcloud079@gmail.com.
